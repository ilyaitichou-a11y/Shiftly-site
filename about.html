About Shiftly

Making connections that click. Shiftly is a short-term jobs platform for international students and small and medium businesses in the Pirkanmaa region.

Our mission

Making work flexible and opportunities accessible.

Why we exist

Many students want flexible work but face language and network barriers. Many local businesses need reliable extra hands on short notice. We bring them together, make sure the work gets done and paid, and build trust through ratings and fast feedback.

Trust

Vetted users, ratings and quick dispute handling.

Energy

Simple, fast, motivating to use.

Openness

Everyone is welcome, whatever their Finnish level.
